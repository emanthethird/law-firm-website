You don't have to know the answer going in — and you won't type a single word. Expertise gives itself away through recognition: the mistake that makes you wince, the jargon that's second nature, the thing you'd reach for without thinking. This instrument shows you concrete options and simply asks which one is most you. The domain your answers keep pointing back to is the answer.

00 How it works

Ten questions. Every answer is already written for you.

  • Round 1 Five questions locate your territory — which of eight broad families of expertise you live in. You just pick the option that fits.
  • Round 2 Five more questions zero in inside that territory, showing you the tell-tale signs of each candidate skill — one per option — so the specific domain surfaces on its own.
  • Verdict The instrument names the single domain your answers converged on, with an honest certainty rating. If they scatter, it says so instead of flattering you.
