From the Founder

We built EMAN because the standard agency model had grown unworthy of its athletes — transactional where it ought to have been considered, loud where it ought to have been precise. Our clients are not catalogue entries. They are singular figures whose careers deserve the same care, taste, and patience as any other piece of long-form work.

A career is roughly fifteen years. Reputation is the remaining fifty. We manage both.

— Emanuel A. Navarro, Founding Partner

§ 02 — Representation

What we
actually do.

Five disciplines, practiced in concert. We are unusual in that the partner who signs you negotiates your contract, drafts your brand strategy, and remains your point of contact a decade later.

I.

Contract
Negotiation

Playing contracts, transfer files, image-rights structures, and exit clauses. We model every offer against the next two markets, not the current one. Forty-one negotiations completed in the last cycle.

Founding
Discipline
II.

Brand &
Endorsements

A portfolio approach. Three to five enduring partnerships rather than thirty short ones. We build briefs, vet creative, and protect the likeness for the back half of a career.

Studio
Practice
III.

Media &
Public Voice

Production, documentary, long-form interviews, ghost-edited columns. Run by a former editor of The Athletic Quarterly. We say no more than we say yes.

Editorial
Standard
IV.

Family Office
& Wealth

In partnership with a Geneva multi-family office. Tax, real estate, philanthropy, and the slow second career most athletes never plan for. Coverage from rookie deal through to retirement plus twenty.

Long
Horizon
V.

Post-Career
Architecture

Board seats, broadcast contracts, founder roles, second-act ownership stakes. We begin this work five years before the final whistle, not the morning after.
