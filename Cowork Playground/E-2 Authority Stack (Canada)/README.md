## Methodology notes

The workspace web_fetch tool is allowlist-restricted and blocks most .gov hosts (uscode.house.gov, ecfr.gov, fam.state.gov, uscis.gov, ustr.gov, travel.state.gov). Pulls were routed through the Claude-in-Chrome MCP, which loads pages in the user's actual browser. Everything marked "verbatim" was extracted from the live agency page on the pull date.

## FAM numbering note

The 9 FAM 402.9 structure listed in the source brief differed from the live FAM as of 02-17-2026. Live structure: 402.9-1 statutory authorities, 402.9-2 overview, 402.9-3 classification codes, 402.9-4 general E-1/E-2 requirements (nationality at 4(B)), 402.9-5 E-1 trader, 402.9-6 E-2 investor (with 6(B) investment, 6(C) real-and-active, 6(D) substantiality, 6(E) marginality, 6(F) develop-and-direct, 6(G) Walsh/Pollard), 402.9-7 employees, 402.9-8 E-3 (Australia), 402.9-9 spouse/children, 402.9-10 country table, 402.9-11 application submission. No standalone 402.9-12. Period-of-stay rules now live in the reciprocity schedule (visa validity/entries), 8 CFR 214.2(e) (DHS authorized stay/extensions), and CBP port-of-entry discretion. The FAM file maps the original brief headings to the live numbering.

## Disclaimer

This library was assembled by automated retrieval on a single date. Authority changes. Verify against current sources before citing in any filing, brief, or client advice. The "verbatim" label means the content matched the live source on the pull date, not that it remains current at the time you read it.

## Truly indispensable (per the source brief)

INA 101(a)(15)(E)(ii), 22 CFR 41.51, 8 CFR 214.2(e), full 9 FAM 402.9, the treaty basis for Canada (USMCA Chapter 16 plus the NAFTA legacy), the DOS reciprocity schedule for Canada, and the Mission Canada / Toronto post instructions. All seven are in this library.
